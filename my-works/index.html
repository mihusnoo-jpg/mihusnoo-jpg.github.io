Formula 1 model

Special items for special occasion! I designed and made this model of a Ferrari Formula 1 within seven days. The project was completed on time and was on display for a leasing company stall in the Engen Motor Show event in Mauritius.

Display products

Materials

Lucite (acrylic)

Signage
