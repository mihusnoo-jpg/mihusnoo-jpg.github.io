Meet Me

“My Work, a Lifetime Passion”

I began my career in 1987 and followed my passion for design as a freelance graphic designer. Since then, I’ve spent the better part of the last two and a half decades working in the design, media, and industrial businesses successfully.

I’ve been around the world seeking more knowledge, I learned and shared the use of several CNC types of equipment in the manufacturing sector. My extensive hands-on experiences and my devotion to perfection allowed me to leap into different leading roles.

My positive and enthusiastic behaviour has enabled me to communicate effectively at all levels. This extensive career reflects a total involvement of my high motivation, leadership qualities, persuasive interaction and communication with people that surround me.

On numerous occasions, I was commended for the consistency of my performance, attention to detail and my ability to solve and prevent many types of issues. I am very attentive to exact specifications requirements as well as cost, quality and time objectives.

I’ve provided training in the sign manufacturing industry where I have successfully managed the implementation and the training of a team of young & fresh recruits to set up a new sign factory of more than 75 personnel in Mauritius.

I believe that while I’m living a passionate life, I have achieved and applied good practices all the way and will always strive to keep the good work for that great feeling of satisfaction.

Since 2017 — a new chapter in Indonesia

My work took me to Indonesia — the country where much of the world’s furniture is made. I took over a furniture business that was drowning, and guided it back to profit: sorting out how the work was organised, how the money was tracked, and how the factory and the showroom talked to each other.

Once the business was healthy, the next weak link was the paperwork itself — orders, production, quality, shipping and wages living in spreadsheets, paper and memory. So I designed and built Veridian, a complete ERP written from scratch, 100% fitted to how we work, to make the business even more efficient.
