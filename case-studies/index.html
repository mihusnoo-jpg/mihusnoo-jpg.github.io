Veridian — an ERP built, not bought

Every ready-made ERP I looked at wanted the business to work the way the software wanted. Renting one would have meant bending my factory, my showroom and my staff around somebody else’s idea of furniture making — paying every month for features we don’t need, and still missing the ones we do.

So I built our own, from an empty folder: a complete business system that runs the factory in Indonesia and the showroom side, exactly the way this business actually works.

The brief

Replace spreadsheets, paper and memory with one system that covers the whole job — from the first quotation to the container on the ship, the money owed, and the staff paid — without depending on the internet, and without a monthly bill to a software company.

What it runs

Sales & orders

  • Quotations, orders and customers
  • Showroom point of sale, stock and returns
  • Credit limits — no interest, no late fees, ever
  • Factory follow-up sheet and production tracking

Production

  • Purchase orders and supplier payables
  • Raw build, finishing, upholstery, glass & mirror
  • Packing with accessory cards and warehouse areas
  • QR labels scanned anywhere on the floor

Quality & shipping

  • QC inspections at every stage, claims and escalations
  • Container proposals, stuffing and loading inspection
  • Shipments, tracking, freight documents, deliveries
  • Suppliers are only paid for work that passed QC

Money & staff

  • Bank, transfers, invoices, payment reminders
  • Payroll, attendance, advances — weekly or monthly
  • Closed months are locked; mistakes are voided, never erased
  • Analytics and a one-click data exporter

Control

  • Two-person rule: nobody approves their own payment
  • Roles, a permission matrix and 186 individual rights
  • Full activity log — who did what, when, from where
  • Two-step sign-in, device approval, session controls

Two sites, one business

  • Factory and showroom each run their own copy of the system
  • Orders, products and alerts travel on one deliberate click
  • Customer details and prices never cross over
  • Each site keeps its own currency and language

How it is built

No internet, ever

The program, its database, the backups and even an offline assistant all run on the local machine. Nothing in the daily work depends on a connection.

A product, not a one-off

Nothing about any country, currency or company is baked in. It ships as an installer that sets up a brand-new computer with a guided, step-by-step first-time setup.

Proven, then locked

Every rule the business depends on is locked with its own automated test, and every change is recorded and restorable. 396 test files guard the work.

Documented in plain words

A full user manual for the staff (every screen, every refusal, every recipe) and a technical manual for the engineers — both written as the system was built.

The result

One system, fitted 100% to how this business works — orders, floor, quality, shipping, money and people — running daily with no monthly bill, no internet requirement, and no data sitting in somebody else’s cloud. Written from scratch, tested screen by screen, now in final rollout.
